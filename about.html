About Hotel Haven

A family-run hotel in Dolly Gunj, Sri Vijaya Puram — clean AC rooms at honest prices.

Our Story & Ethos

Genuine Island Hospitality

Hotel Haven started as a small family business and has stayed that way. We run 35 clean, air-conditioned rooms at honest prices, and we look after our guests ourselves — the person who answers your call is usually the person you'll meet at reception.

We're on Govt. Polytechnic Road in Dolly Gunj, a quiet part of town that's still close to everything — 2.5 km from Veer Savarkar International Airport and 4 km from Haddo Harbour, where ferries leave for Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil).

Our front desk sorts the rest — Cellular Jail tickets, ferry and boat bookings, and scooters on rent if you'd rather explore on your own.
