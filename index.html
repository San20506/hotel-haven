Welcome to Hotel Haven

Comfort
without
pretense.

Govt. Polytechnic Road, Dolly Gunj — quiet neighbourhood, close to everything.

We started as a small family business and stayed that way. The person who answers your call at 8 am is usually the person you'll meet at reception — no call centre, no chatbot.

We're on Govt. Polytechnic Road in Dolly Gunj — 2.5 km from Veer Savarkar International Airport, 4 km from Haddo Harbour where ferries leave for Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil), and 3.5 km from Aberdeen Bazaar and Cellular Jail. Close enough to walk to your ferry, quiet enough to actually sleep.

Every room has AC, a geyser, intercom and free Wi-Fi. The restaurant does Indian, Chinese and continental (seafood when it's in season). We rent scooters, arrange airport pickups, and sort ferry and Cellular Jail tickets at the front desk.

Honest answers

Common
questions.

What are your meal plans?+

CP (breakfast included), MAP (breakfast & dinner) and AP (all meals). Prices depend on room type — see each room's tariff table for exact numbers.

Do you offer airport pickup?+

Yes — chargeable pickup & drop on request. Share your flight number and we'll be there. Scooters and full island tours can also be arranged.

What facilities are on site?+

Restaurant, free Wi-Fi, intercom in every room, 24-hour water, geysers in every bathroom, laundry on call, two-wheeler rentals and tour assistance.

How far are the main sights?+

Cellular Jail & Aberdeen Bazaar 3.5 km, Haddo Harbour 4 km, airport 2.5 km. Ferries to Havelock/Neil leave from Haddo — we book them for you.
